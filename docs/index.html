truckee-flights

An independent, auditable record of flight activity at Truckee Tahoe Airport, maintained from within the community it serves. Not affiliated with the airport district, the town, or any aircraft operator. Built from public data, with open code and data.

Flight data can establish who flies here, when, and from where. It cannot settle whether the current volume of jet traffic is what this community wants from a high-Sierra airport that also serves glider pilots, recreational flyers, and local emergency services. That is a community question. The purpose of this site is that it gets discussed over shared, checkable facts.

Quiet hours, in plain terms

The airport asks all aircraft not to fly between 10:00 pm and 7:00 am (the Fly Quiet program). Federal law (the Airport Noise and Capacity Act of 1990) and the assurances attached to accepted FAA grants together keep the district from making this mandatory, so it is a request, backed by lease incentives rather than enforcement. The program also defines Shoulder Hour Protocols for flights that need the edges of the quiet window; the log records all movements regardless. The quiet-hours log above records every takeoff and landing during the monitored portion of quiet hours, including air ambulances and other working flights.

Tracking resources and their limits

  • ADS-B Exchange
    The tracker this site is built on. Shows effectively all transmitting aircraft, including those that have opted out of display elsewhere. Less polished than commercial trackers.
  • FlightAware and Flightradar24
    Easier to use, but they honor FAA display-blocking requests (LADD). Some aircraft that use this airport will not appear on them: in Study 1, 7 of the 59 aircraft that operated here were blocked, including two of the four aircraft with the most quiet-hours operations that week. If you look up a flight and find nothing, that is a known limit of those sites, not proof the flight did not happen.
  • Airport district comment portal
    The district's official channel for noise comments (PlaneNoise). Comments become part of the district's own reporting to its board.
  • Truckee Tahoe Airport District
    Operations statistics, board meetings, and the noise abatement procedures pilots are asked to follow.
  • Town of Truckee General Plan, Noise Element
    The town's planning framework for noise, including aircraft noise.
